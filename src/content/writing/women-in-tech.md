Women have made significant strides in the technology industry over the years, but they still face challenges and barriers to success. As a woman who has spent her career in technology, here are seven things I'd offer to anyone navigating this path.

### 1. Believe in yourself

The first step to success is believing in yourself. Don't let anyone else's opinions or prejudices hold you back. You have the skills and the talent to succeed in tech, so don't be afraid to take risks and pursue your goals.

### 2. Build a support network

Building a strong support network is critical to success in any field, but it's especially important in tech where women are often underrepresented. Find mentors and peers who can offer guidance, support, and advice as you navigate your career. Attend industry events, join online communities, and participate in networking opportunities to connect with others in your field.

### 3. Be proactive

Don't wait for opportunities to come to you — create them yourself. Look for ways to contribute to your company and the tech industry as a whole. Volunteer for projects, take on extra responsibilities, and seek out leadership opportunities. By being proactive, you can demonstrate your skills and make a name for yourself.

### 4. Embrace continuous learning

The tech industry is constantly evolving, so it's essential to keep your skills up-to-date. Attend training sessions, workshops, and conferences to learn about new technologies and trends. Consider pursuing advanced degrees or certifications to enhance your knowledge and expertise.

### 5. Speak up

Women are often underrepresented in meetings and other professional settings. Don't be afraid to speak up and share your ideas and opinions. Your perspective is valuable, and your input can make a difference in the success of your team and your company.

### 6. Advocate for diversity and inclusion

You have the power to advocate for diversity and inclusion in your workplace and the industry as a whole. Speak out against bias and discrimination and work to create a more inclusive culture in your workplace.

### 7. Stay resilient

The tech industry can be challenging, but don't let setbacks discourage you. Stay resilient, persevere through challenges, and learn from your mistakes. Your determination and resilience will help you achieve your goals.

Finally, always remember — the first person who has to believe in your success is *you*.
