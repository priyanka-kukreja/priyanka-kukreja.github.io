HEADLINE
Technical roots with Product mindset


--- PARAGRAPH 1 (current role) ---
I'm the Product Leader at CodeRabbit, where I own the success of Issue Planner (one of CodeRabbit's flagship 0→1 bets) working directly with the COO to build this category-defining product. Issue Planner is designed to redefine how engineering teams move from human intent to AI-generated code: taking a ticket, a goal, or an ambiguous instruction, and turning it into a structured, reviewable plan that an AI agent can execute.

--- PARAGRAPH 2 (What I think about) ---
The questions that keep me up: How do you take an AI capability from demo to durable product? How do you stage a 0→1 roadmap without losing the magic? What does great product leadership look like in a world where the models change every quarter? I write about these here, along with strategy, technology, and the occasional note on the long arc of a career.

--- PARAGRAPH 3 (engineering background) ---
Before product, I spent close to a decade on the engineering side. At Stripe, I led a team of engineers building financial infrastructure that moves money across borders at internet scale ($X billion each day). At Meta, I built AI-powered integrity systems tackling harmful content, fraud, and scams at massive scale. (The Indian Express profiled my work on making the virtual world safer). Earlier in my career, I worked at Microsoft on developer and cloud
automation, and at GoDaddy on domain pricing systems.

--- PARAGRAPH 4 (education) ---
I hold a Master's in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University, where I specialized in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I also hold a Bachelor's in Computer Science and Master's in Economics from BITS - Pilani. This dual foundation in technology and business enables me to build products that are truly delightful to customers and shareholders alike.
